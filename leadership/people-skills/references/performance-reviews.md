# Performance reviews

Assumes the base frameworks in SKILL.md (Carnegie, OCEAN, Radical Candor) are already applied.

## Structure a review before delivering it

- Lead with the clearest signal, not a chronological list of everything that happened this period - reviews that try to be complete end up flat; reviews that lead with the one thing that most determines the next period land.
- Specific, observed behavior with a concrete example beats a trait label every time ("in the migration review you cut off two people mid-sentence" beats "you can be dismissive"). Trait labels trigger defensiveness (Carnegie) and are also just less actionable.
- Separate "what happened" from "what it means for level/comp/promotion" as two distinct parts of the conversation - collapsing them makes the person stop listening to the behavioral feedback the moment they've heard the outcome.

## Calibration (when there is a group calibration step)

- Calibrate on evidence, not on how recently or vividly you remember an example (recency bias is the single most common distortion in review cycles) - keep a running note through the period, don't reconstruct from memory at review time.
- If your read of someone is unusually harsh or unusually generous relative to peers in calibration, that is information about your own blind spot on that person (possibly an OCEAN mismatch you haven't named to yourself) before it is information about them.

## Delivering the hard version

- The Radical Candor failure mode to watch for here specifically is Ruinous Empathy: softening a rating or a message so much that the person leaves the room without understanding there's a real problem. If they're surprised by a low rating, the review conversation was not the first time the feedback should have surfaced - name that as a process fix for next cycle, not just a one-off miss.
- Give the person room to respond before moving to next steps - a review that is entirely one-directional reads as a verdict being handed down rather than a conversation, regardless of how accurate the content is.
- Write the documentation in behavioral language before the conversation, not after - if you can't write the specific example down, the feedback isn't concrete enough to deliver yet.
